🧟 ZOMBIE APOCALYPSE

Seven browser games. No installs. Pick your way to survive the outbreak.
🔫
TWIN-STICK SHOOTER

Zombie Survival

Top-down endless waves. WASD to move, mouse to aim & fire. Grab cash, dash through hordes, and buy upgrades between waves. Runners and brutes get nastier the longer you last.
🏚️
BASE DEFENSE

Last Barricade

Side-scrolling last stand. Hold the wall against waves from the right. Spend scrap on turrets, spikes, and repairs. Don't let them reach the base.
🔦
STEALTH MAZE

Outbreak Escape

Navigate a dark infected building by flashlight. Find every keycard, reach the exit — and keep quiet. Sprinting is fast but loud, and noise wakes the dead.
🌱
LANE DEFENSE

Garden of the Dead

Plant shooters, sunflowers, walls and bombs across 5 lanes to stop zombies reaching your house. Gather sun, manage your economy, clear 10 escalating waves.
🏃
ENDLESS RUNNER

Dead Run

Outrun the horde on your heels. Jump wrecks & pits, slide under signs, grab coins and adrenaline dashes. One hit and they catch you — distance is everything.
🎯
SCOPE DEFENSE

Rooftop Sniper

Pick zombies off through a rifle scope before they reach the survivors' door. Headshots are one-shot kills, combos multiply your score, and ammo is limited. Zoom & breathe.
🛖
SURVIVAL MANAGEMENT

Shelter 7

Run an underground shelter. Assign survivors to forage, scavenge and guard by day, then repel the night raid. Build defenses, ration food & water, last 30 days.
7 games · HTML5 Canvas · zero dependencies · WASD + mouse / touch supported
