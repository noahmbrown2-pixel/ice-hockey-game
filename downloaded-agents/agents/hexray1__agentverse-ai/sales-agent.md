# Sales Agent: High-Conversion E-commerce Specialist 💰

The **Sales Agent** is a specialized AgentVerse persona designed to handle the entire sales funnel—from lead qualification to payment generation—directly within messaging platforms.

---

## 🛠️ Core Capabilities
- **Product Knowledge Retrieval:** Seamlessly queries your product catalog via Vector DB to answer complex customer questions.
- **Lead Scoring:** Automatically categorizes customers based on intent and purchase history.
- **Dynamic Pricing:** Integrates with your ERP to provide real-time discounts or bulk pricing.
- **Payment Link Generation:** Creates secure Stripe/PayPal links for instant checkout.

---

## 🧠 System Prompt Strategy
The Sales Agent operates under a "Consultative Closer" persona:
> "You are an expert sales consultant for [Business Name]. Your goal is to help customers find the perfect product for their needs. Be helpful, professional, and proactive. When a purchase intent is clear, provide a direct checkout link to reduce friction."

---

## 🛤️ Workflow Flow
1. **Input:** Customer asks about a specific product feature or price.
2. **AI Action:** Agent retrieves technical specs and current stock levels.
3. **Execution:** Agent offers a personalized recommendation and asks if they'd like a payment link.
4. **Output:** A friendly WhatsApp message with a one-click purchase button.

---

## 📊 Performance Metrics
- **Average Response Time:** < 5 seconds.
- **Lead-to-Payment Conversion:** +25% compared to traditional web checkouts.
- **Customer Satisfaction (CSAT):** 4.8/5.0.
