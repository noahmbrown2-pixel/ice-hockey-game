# KB Article Writing Skill

Auto-triggered skill for creating knowledge base articles.

## Trigger Phrases

- "write article"
- "knowledge base"
- "documentation"
- "help article"
- "create guide"

## Article Types

### How-To Article
Step-by-step instructions for completing a task

### Troubleshooting Article
Diagnose and resolve specific issues

### FAQ Article
Quick answers to common questions

### Reference Article
Feature documentation and specifications

### Policy Article
Terms, guidelines, and policies

## Article Templates

- **{Issue}**: {Solution}

- [{Article Title}](link)

*Last updated: {date} | Article ID: {id}*

## Writing Guidelines

### Tone
- Clear and concise
- Active voice
- Second person ("you")
- Friendly but professional

### Structure
- Scannable headings
- Numbered steps for procedures
- Bullet points for lists
- Callouts for important info

### Visuals
- Screenshots for UI steps
- Diagrams for concepts
- GIFs for complex interactions

## Quality Checklist

```markdown
## Article Review Checklist

### Content
- [ ] Solves stated problem
- [ ] Steps are accurate and tested
- [ ] No assumptions about user knowledge
- [ ] Includes all prerequisites

### Structure
- [ ] Clear, descriptive title
- [ ] Logical heading hierarchy
- [ ] Numbered steps where appropriate
- [ ] Related articles linked

### Style
- [ ] Active voice
- [ ] Concise sentences
- [ ] No jargon without explanation
- [ ] Consistent terminology

### Technical
- [ ] Screenshots current
- [ ] Links working
- [ ] Correct product/feature names
- [ ] Version-specific info noted
```

## Maintenance

### Review Triggers
- Product update affecting article
- Customer feedback indicates confusion
- Support tickets referencing article issues
- 90+ days since last review
