# Empathy Patterns Skill

Auto-triggered skill for handling difficult customer interactions.

## Trigger Phrases

- "frustrated customer"
- "angry"
- "upset"
- "empathy"
- "tone"
- "de-escalate"

## Empathy Framework

### The LEAP Method
- **L**isten: Let customer fully express concern
- **E**mpathize: Acknowledge their feelings
- **A**pologize: When appropriate
- **P**roblem-solve: Move toward resolution

## Response Patterns by Sentiment

### For Frustrated Customers

**Opening Statements**:
```
✅ "I completely understand how frustrating this must be, and I'm sorry
   you're dealing with this."

✅ "Thank you for your patience while we work through this. I can see
   this has been a difficult experience."

✅ "I hear you, and I want you to know that resolving this is my top
   priority right now."
```

**Validation Phrases**:
```
✅ "You're absolutely right to be concerned about this."
✅ "I would feel the same way in your situation."
✅ "That's not the experience we want you to have."
```

### For Angry Customers

**De-escalation Opening**:
```
✅ "I can hear how upset you are, and I want to help make this right."

✅ "I'm truly sorry for what you've experienced. Let me focus on
   getting this resolved for you right away."

✅ "This is clearly not acceptable, and I appreciate you bringing it
   to our attention. Let's fix this together."
```

**Avoid**:
```
❌ "Calm down"
❌ "As I already explained..."
❌ "That's not our policy"
❌ "There's nothing I can do"
❌ "You should have..."
```

### For Confused Customers

**Clarifying Statements**:
```
✅ "Let me make sure I understand correctly..."
✅ "I want to walk you through this step by step."
✅ "That's a great question - let me explain..."
```

## Apology Patterns

### When to Apologize
- Service failure or outage
- Mistake on our part
- Customer inconvenienced
- Poor previous interaction

### How to Apologize
```
Specific: "I apologize for the delay in responding to your ticket."
Not: "Sorry for any inconvenience."

Ownership: "I'm sorry we didn't catch this issue sooner."
Not: "I'm sorry you feel that way."

Action-oriented: "I apologize for this error. I've corrected it and
here's what happens next..."
```

### When Not to Apologize
- Customer error (guide, don't blame)
- Policy explanation (explain value)
- Feature requests (acknowledge, set expectations)

## Tone Adjustment Guide

| Sentiment | Tone | Priority | Structure |
|-----------|------|----------|-----------|
| Positive | Friendly, casual | Standard | Brief, helpful |
| Neutral | Professional | Standard | Clear, efficient |
| Concerned | Reassuring | Elevated | Detailed, thorough |
| Frustrated | Empathetic, focused | High | Solution-first |
| Angry | Calm, action-oriented | Urgent | De-escalate, resolve |

## Bridge Phrases

Transition from empathy to solution:
```
"Now that I understand the situation, here's what I can do..."
"Let me take care of this for you right now..."
"I want to make sure we resolve this completely. Here's our plan..."
```

## Closing Patterns

**After Resolution**:
```
"I'm glad we could get this sorted out for you. Please don't hesitate
to reach out if anything else comes up."
```

**After Partial Resolution**:
```
"I've started the process on our end. I'll personally follow up with
you by [date] with an update."
```

**After Unable to Resolve**:
```
"I understand this isn't the answer you were hoping for. Here's what
we can do instead... / Here's who can help further..."
```
