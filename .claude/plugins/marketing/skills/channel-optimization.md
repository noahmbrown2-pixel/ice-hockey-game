## Channel-Specific Best Practices

### Email Marketing

**Optimization Levers**:
| Element | Best Practice | Test Variations |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| Subject Line | <50 chars, personalized | A/B test 2-3 |
| Preview Text | Complements subject | Match tone |
| Send Time | Tue-Thu, 10am or 2pm | Test by segment |
| From Name | Personal vs brand | Test both |
| CTA | Single, clear, above fold | Button vs text |

**Key Metrics**:
- Open Rate: 20-25% (B2B), 15-20% (B2C)
- Click Rate: 2-5%
- Unsubscribe: <0.5%

### Social Media

**Platform Optimization**:

**LinkedIn**:
- Post length: 1,300+ characters for algorithm
- Best time: Tue-Thu, 7-8am or 5-6pm
- Content: Professional insights, industry news
- Engagement: Comments > Likes
- Hashtags: 3-5, industry-specific

**X/Twitter**:
- Post length: Use full 280 when valuable
- Best time: 8-10am, 12pm, 4-6pm
- Content: Quick insights, engagement hooks
- Engagement: Replies, retweets
- Hashtags: 1-2 max

**Facebook**:
- Post length: <80 chars gets 66% more engagement
- Best time: 1-4pm, Wed-Thu
- Content: Stories, videos, community
- Engagement: Shares > Comments > Likes

**Instagram**:
- Caption: First line is crucial
- Best time: 11am-1pm, 7-9pm
- Content: Visual-first, behind-the-scenes
- Hashtags: 11-15 in first comment
- Stories: 1-7 per day

### Paid Search (PPC)

**Optimization Areas**:
```
Quality Score Improvement:
├── Keyword Relevance
│   └── Match keywords to ad copy and landing page
├── Landing Page Experience
│   └── Fast, relevant, mobile-friendly
└── Expected CTR
    └── Compelling ad copy with keywords
```

**Ad Copy Structure**:
- Headline 1: Primary keyword + benefit
- Headline 2: Differentiator or CTA
- Headline 3: Brand or trust element
- Description: Features, proof, CTA

### SEO

**On-Page Priorities**:
1. Title tag optimization
2. Meta description (CTR focus)
3. H1-H6 hierarchy with keywords
4. Content depth and quality
5. Internal linking
6. Image optimization
7. Page speed

**Content Strategy**:
- Target 1 primary keyword per page
- Include 3-5 related keywords naturally
- Answer search intent comprehensively
- Update content quarterly

## Cross-Channel Attribution

### Models
- Last Click: Credit final touchpoint
- First Click: Credit discovery point
- Linear: Equal credit all touches
- Time Decay: More credit to recent
- Position-Based: 40/20/40 first/middle/last

### Recommended Approach
Use multi-touch attribution for full picture, but understand last-click for immediate optimization.
