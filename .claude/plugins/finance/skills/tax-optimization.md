### Key Tax Concepts

| Concept | Description | Benefit |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| Section 179 | Immediate equipment expensing | Accelerate deductions |
| Bonus Depreciation | Additional first-year depreciation | Accelerate deductions |
| QSBS | Qualified Small Business Stock | Capital gains exclusion |
| R&D Credit | Research & Development credit | Dollar-for-dollar reduction |
| NOL Carryforward | Net Operating Loss | Offset future income |
