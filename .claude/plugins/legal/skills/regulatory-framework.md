## Key Regulations

### Data Privacy Regulations

#### GDPR (EU)
**Scope**: Any organization processing EU residents' data

**Key Requirements**:
- Lawful basis for processing
- Data subject rights (access, erasure, portability)
- Data protection by design
- Breach notification (72 hours)
- DPO appointment (certain cases)
- Records of processing
- International transfer restrictions

**Penalties**: Up to €20M or 4% global revenue

#### CCPA/CPRA (California)
**Scope**: Businesses meeting thresholds serving CA residents

**Key Requirements**:
- Right to know
- Right to delete
- Right to opt-out of sale
- Non-discrimination
- Privacy notices

**Penalties**: $2,500-$7,500 per violation

#### Other Privacy Laws
| Jurisdiction | Law | Key Difference |
|--------------|-----|----------------|
