# Sales Methodology Skill

Auto-triggered skill for structured sales qualification and process.

## Trigger Phrases

- "MEDDIC"
- "BANT"
- "sales process"
- "qualification"
- "discovery questions"

## Qualification Frameworks

### MEDDIC

```markdown
## MEDDIC Qualification: {Opportunity}

### M - Metrics
**Question**: "What metrics are you trying to improve?"
**Current State**: {baseline metrics}
**Desired State**: {target metrics}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜ (1-3)

### E - Economic Buyer
**Question**: "Who has the final authority to approve this purchase?"
**Identified**: {name and title}
**Engaged**: Yes/No
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### D - Decision Criteria
**Question**: "What criteria will you use to evaluate solutions?"
**Technical**: {requirements}
**Business**: {requirements}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### D - Decision Process
**Question**: "Walk me through your evaluation and approval process"
**Steps**: {identified steps}
**Timeline**: {dates}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### I - Identify Pain
**Question**: "What challenges are you experiencing? What's the impact?"
**Pains**: {identified pains}
**Impact**: {quantified impact}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### C - Champion
**Question**: "Who internally is advocating for this project?"
**Champion**: {name}
**Access**: {level of access}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### Overall Score: {X}/18
**Recommendation**: {Pursue/Nurture/Disqualify}
```

### BANT

```markdown
## BANT Qualification: {Opportunity}

### B - Budget

**Status**: {Allocated/Identified/Unknown/None}
**Amount**: {if known}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### A - Authority
**Question**: "Who makes the final decision on purchases like this?"
**Decision Maker**: {name/title}
**Access Level**: {Direct/Through Champion/None}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### N - Need
**Question**: "What problem are you trying to solve?"
**Pain Level**: {Critical/Important/Nice-to-have}
**Urgency**: {Immediate/This Quarter/Someday}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### T - Timeline
**Question**: "When are you looking to have a solution in place?"
**Target Date**: {date}
**Driving Event**: {what's creating urgency}
**Score**: ⬜⬜⬜

### Overall Score: {X}/12
```

## Discovery Question Bank

### Opening Questions
1. "What prompted you to take this meeting?"
2. "What's changed that makes this a priority now?"
3. "How long has this been a challenge?"

### Pain Questions
1. "What's the biggest challenge you face with {area}?"
2. "How does this impact your team day-to-day?"
3. "What have you tried before to address this?"

### Impact Questions
1. "What happens if this problem continues?"
2. "How does this affect your broader goals?"
3. "What does this cost you in time/money/opportunity?"

### Vision Questions
1. "What would success look like?"
2. "How would your team's work change?"
3. "What would you be able to do that you can't today?"

### Process Questions
1. "Walk me through your typical buying process"
2. "Who else needs to be involved in this decision?"
3. "What could derail this initiative?"

## Sales Stage Definitions

| Stage | Criteria | Exit Requirements |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Lead | Initial interest | Qualified need identified |
| Discovery | Active evaluation | MEDDIC 12+/18 or BANT 8+/12 |
| Demo | Solution fit confirmed | Technical validation |
| Proposal | Business case built | Budget confirmed |
| Negotiation | Terms discussion | All stakeholders aligned |
| Closed Won | Contract signed | ✅ |
| Closed Lost | Opportunity ended | ❌ Loss reason documented |
