# Competitor Positioning Skill

Auto-triggered skill for competitive differentiation.

## Trigger Phrases

- "vs competitor"
- "differentiation"
- "why us"
- "competitive"
- "how do we compare"

## Positioning Framework

### Battle Card Format

```markdown
## Competitor Battle Card: {Competitor Name}

### Quick Stats
| Metric | Them | Us |
|--------|------|-----|
| Founded | {year} | {year} |
| Size | {employees} | {employees} |
| Funding | {amount} | {amount} |
| Customers | {count} | {count} |

### Their Positioning
"{Their tagline or positioning statement}"

### Where They Win
- {Strength 1}
- {Strength 2}
- {Strength 3}

### Where We Win
- {Our advantage 1}
- {Our advantage 2}
- {Our advantage 3}

### Common Objections

**"They're bigger/more established"**
> Response: "Size doesn't always mean better fit. We focus on {segment}
> and deliver {specific advantage}. Our customers chose us because..."

**"They have feature X"**
> Response: "We approach that differently. Instead of X, we provide Y
> which actually addresses the underlying need of..."

**"They're cheaper"**
> Response: "Let's look at total value. When you factor in {hidden costs},
> our customers actually see {better outcomes}..."

### Landmines to Plant
Questions to ask that highlight competitor weaknesses:
1. "How important is {area where we're stronger}?"
2. "Have you experienced {known competitor issue}?"
3. "What happens when you need {thing they can't do}?"

### Proof Points
- {Customer who switched from them to us}
- {Head-to-head win story}
- {Analyst quote favoring us}
```

### Quick Comparison Matrix

```markdown
## Quick Comparison: Us vs {Competitor}

| Capability | Us | Them | Notes |
|------------|:--:|:----:|-------|
| Core Feature 1 | ✅ | ✅ | We have deeper {X} |
| Core Feature 2 | ✅ | ⚠️ | They require add-on |
| Advanced 1 | ✅ | ❌ | Unique to us |
| Integration | ✅ | ✅ | We have native; they use partner |
| Support | ✅ | ⚠️ | We include; they charge extra |
| Pricing | $$ | $$$ | We're 20% lower on average |

✅ = Strong | ⚠️ = Partial/Limited | ❌ = Doesn't have
```

## Competitive Scenarios

### They're Incumbent
- Focus on: Innovation, modern approach, switching costs
- Avoid: Criticizing directly, feature-by-feature comparison
- Strategy: Land with a specific use case, expand

### We're Incumbent
- Focus on: Relationship, expanded value, switching risk
- Avoid: Complacency, dismissing concerns
- Strategy: Deepen engagement, showcase roadmap

### Head-to-Head Evaluation
- Focus on: Differentiation, proof points, champion enablement
- Avoid: FUD (Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt)
- Strategy: Arm your champion with ammunition

### They're Not in Deal (Yet)
- Focus on: Building urgency, showing complete solution
- Avoid: Bringing them up unnecessarily
- Strategy: Position against status quo, not competitor

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Know competitor products well
- Focus on customer value
- Use data and proof points
- Respect the competitor

### Don't
- Trash talk competitors
- Make unverified claims
- Get defensive
- Assume customer knows differences
