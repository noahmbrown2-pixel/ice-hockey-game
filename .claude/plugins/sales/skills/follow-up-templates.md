# Follow-Up Templates Skill

Auto-triggered skill for post-meeting communications.

## Trigger Phrases

- "follow up email"
- "next steps"
- "recap email"
- "thank you"
- "meeting summary"

## Template Categories

### Post-Discovery Call

```markdown
Subject: Great connecting - {Topic} next steps

Hi {Name},

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about {topic/challenge}.

**Key Takeaways:**
- {Pain point 1 discussed}
- {Pain point 2 discussed}
- {Goal they mentioned}

**Agreed Next Steps:**
- [ ] {Action item 1} - {Owner} by {Date}
- [ ] {Action item 2} - {Owner} by {Date}

As promised, I'm attaching {resource mentioned}. I think you'll find the
section on {specific topic} particularly relevant to your situation with
{their challenge}.

Looking forward to our next conversation on {date/topic}.

Best,
{Your name}
```

### Post-Demo Follow-Up

```markdown
Subject: {Product} demo follow-up + next steps

Hi {Name},

Thank you for your time today reviewing {Product}. Based on our discussion,
I wanted to recap the key points and outline next steps.

**What We Covered:**
- {Feature 1}: How it addresses {their need}
- {Feature 2}: Solving {their challenge}
- {Integration point}: Connecting with {their system}

**Questions You Raised:**
- {Question 1}: {Brief answer/promised follow-up}
- {Question 2}: {Brief answer/promised follow-up}

**Next Steps:**
1. I'll send {promised materials} by {date}
2. You'll {their action item}
3. We'll reconnect on {date} to {purpose}

Please don't hesitate to reach out if any questions come up in the meantime.

Best,
{Your name}
```

### Proposal Follow-Up

```markdown
Subject: Proposal for {Company} - {Solution}

Hi {Name},

As discussed, please find attached our proposal for {solution/project}.

**Proposal Highlights:**
- Investment: {price}
- Timeline: {implementation timeline}
- Key deliverables: {summary}

**Why Now:**
Based on our conversations, implementing by {date} would help you achieve
{their goal} before {their deadline/event}.

I'd love to schedule a call to walk through the details and answer any
questions. Would {date/time} or {date/time} work for you and {other stakeholder}?

Looking forward to your feedback.

Best,
{Your name}
```

### Check-In (Gone Dark)

```markdown
Subject: Quick check-in on {Project/Initiative}

Hi {Name},

I wanted to follow up on our conversation from {date} about {topic}.

I know things get busy, so I wanted to check in and see if:
- Your priorities have shifted?
- You need any additional information?
- There's a better time to reconnect?

If {project} is still on your radar, I'd be happy to {offer - e.g.,
"schedule a brief call" or "send updated information"}.

Either way, I'd appreciate a quick update when you have a moment.

Best,
{Your name}
```

### Post-Objection Follow-Up

```markdown
Subject: Following up on your {concern}

Hi {Name},

Thank you for sharing your concerns about {objection topic} on our call.
I wanted to follow up with some additional information.

**Addressing Your Concern:**
{Detailed response to their objection}

**What Others Have Done:**
{Customer example who had similar concern and outcome}

**Additional Resources:**
- {Case study link}
- {ROI calculator/data}

Would it be helpful to connect you with {reference customer} who faced
a similar situation? They'd be happy to share their experience.

Let me know your thoughts.

Best,
{Your name}
```

### Meeting Request (Cold)

```markdown
Subject: {Mutual connection} suggested I reach out / Quick question about {their initiative}

Hi {Name},

{Personalized opening based on research - recent news, mutual connection, their content}

I work with {similar companies/role} who are focused on {outcome}.
{One sentence on relevant achievement with similar customer}.

Would you be open to a brief conversation about how {your company} is
approaching {their likely challenge}?

I have time {day/time} or {day/time} - or happy to work around your schedule.

Best,
{Your name}

P.S. {Optional - additional personalization or value add}
```

## Best Practices

### Timing
- Send within 24 hours of meeting
- Follow up on commitments same day
- Check-ins: 3-5-7 day cadence

### Structure
- Clear subject line with context
- Recap key points immediately
- Specific next steps with owners and dates
- Single clear call-to-action

### Personalization
- Reference specific topics discussed
- Connect to their stated goals
- Avoid generic templates
