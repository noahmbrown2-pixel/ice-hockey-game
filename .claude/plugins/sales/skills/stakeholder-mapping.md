# Stakeholder Mapping Skill

Auto-triggered skill for identifying and analyzing decision makers.

## Trigger Phrases

- "decision makers"
- "stakeholders"
- "who to contact"
- "org chart"
- "buying committee"

## Stakeholder Framework

### RACI Model for B2B Sales
```
R - Responsible (Day-to-day users/implementers)
A - Accountable (Budget holder, final decision)
C - Consulted (Technical review, security, legal)
I - Informed (Executives who need awareness)
```

### Typical Enterprise Buying Committee

```markdown
## Stakeholder Map: {Company Name}

### Decision Makers

#### Economic Buyer (A)
**Likely Title**: {VP/Director of X}
**Role**: Signs the check, owns budget
**Concerns**: ROI, risk, strategic fit
**Approach**: Business value, executive summary

#### Technical Buyer (C)
**Likely Title**: {CTO/Architect/IT Director}
**Role**: Evaluates technical fit
**Concerns**: Integration, security, scalability
**Approach**: Technical deep-dive, architecture review

#### User Buyer (R)
**Likely Title**: {Manager/Team Lead}
**Role**: Daily user, champion
**Concerns**: Ease of use, workflow fit, support
**Approach**: Demo, trial, peer references

#### Coach/Champion
**Likely Title**: {varies}
**Role**: Internal advocate
**Concerns**: Making good recommendation
**Approach**: Enable with materials, regular updates

### Influencers

| Role | Influence | Typical Concerns |
|------|-----------|------------------|
| Legal | Can block | Contracts, liability |
| Security | Can block | Compliance, data handling |
| Procurement | Can delay | Pricing, terms |
| Finance | Can block | Budget, ROI proof |
```

## Intelligence Sources

### LinkedIn Signals
- Title and tenure
- Career progression
- Shared connections
- Content engagement

### Company Signals
- Recent hires in relevant areas
- Org announcements
- New initiatives mentioned

### Engagement History
- Previous interactions
- Email opens/clicks
- Content downloads
