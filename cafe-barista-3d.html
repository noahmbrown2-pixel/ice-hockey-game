Cafe Barista 3D

You're behind the counter, first-person. Customers walk up with an order. Each item needs the right equipment used in the right order before the clock runs out. Bigger orders mean more steps and more time — but the margin gets tighter every day.

How to Play

  • WASD to walk, mouse to look around. Click to lock the mouse for looking, and click again to use whatever equipment is under your crosshair.
  • A glowing gold beacon shoots up from the equipment you need next, and a chain of floor arrows plus an on-screen compass arrow point straight at it.
  • Some steps ask you to pick an ingredient — match the milk / syrup / toast called out in the item name.
  • Wrong equipment costs you seconds. Running out loses the customer and hurts your reputation.
  • Survive 7 days as Cafe Legend. Reputation hits 0 and the cafe closes for good.
  • Press Esc to release the mouse and pause mid-order.
