ROOFTOP SNIPER

Scope defense. Zombies cross the street toward the survivors' door at the bottom. Pick them off from your rooftop.
Aim: Mouse   Fire: Click   Reload: R   Zoom: Right-click / Z
Headshots (the small circle) are one-shot kills and worth more. You have limited ammo per mag — make them count. Protect the door's HP.
Watch for armored tank zombies at higher waves — they soak many body shots (a headshot still drops them), so aim for the head.
Spend score to deploy auto-turrets that flank the door and shred the horde for you: press 1 (or T) — 150 pts each. Then 2 (or U) to upgrade all turrets (more damage + faster fire, up to Lv5). Turrets overheat under sustained fire and briefly shut down to cool — watch the heat bar.
