SHELTER 7

Survival management. You run an underground shelter. Each day, assign your survivors to jobs, then survive the night raid.
Food 🍖 keeps people alive · Water 💧 too · Scrap 🔧 builds defenses · Defense 🛡 repels raids · Medicine 💊 heals.
Click a survivor then a job to assign. Build upgrades with scrap. Click END DAY to face the night. Keep everyone fed and the walls strong. How many days can you last?
